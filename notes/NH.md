Carroll County. I expected it to be
Coos County, in the Presidential range.

But this fact highlights that it is the densest collection
of records that HAVE latitude and longitude values,
AND that make it into CLH.

This emphasizes that these maps do NOT show
where lichens are, but show where lichens have 
been collected and have adequate recorded and transmitted
data to be included in this dataset.

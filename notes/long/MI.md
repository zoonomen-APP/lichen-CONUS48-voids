# Michigan's apex cell: the longer story

Companion to the short note beside Michigan's sheet. Begun 2026-11-02; added to as understanding changes, with each addition dated. Counts are from clh_2026_08 as of 2026-11-02 (cell 121441, 3,270 records), after J. A. Malachowski's names were regularized.

The history told here is inferred from the CLH records alone: dates, collectors, herbaria, coordinates and catalogue numbers, read as they stand in the snapshot. Other sources, such as field notebooks, publications, herbarium archives, or the recollections of people who were there, may add to it or alter it, and could show parts of it to be false.

## Where

Every record in the cell lies between 48.03 and 48.15 N and between 88.56 and 88.78 W, the middle of Isle Royale in Lake Superior, which is part of Keweenaw County, Michigan, and of Isle Royale National Park. The county field reads Keweenaw_Co. (3,196) or Isle_Royale_NP (69); three are blank and two name counties in southern Michigan (see "The Imshaug packets"). The records sit on 189 distinct points. The island's long, narrow shape may add to the concentration, since collecting spread along it can fall into only a few cells of this grid (a reading of the grid, not something the records show).

## Wetmore

C. M. Wetmore collected 2,215 of the 3,270 records (68%), but not in one campaign. By year: 1957 115, 1958 142, 1959 337 (594 together), 1972 149 (all on one day, below), 1980 88, 1983 1,050, 1984 225, 1999 105, and 2 undated. 1983 alone is a third of the cell: mostly July (1983-07-02, 07-10, 07-11 and 07-13 each over 100 records), 1,014 of the 1,050 held at MIN. The block's 1,363 records for the 1980s are essentially that summer. His records are spread over MIN (1,580 of the cell's records are at MIN), MSC, and many other herbaria.

## Other episodes

1930. A party of A. H. Povah, C. A. Brown, J. B. McFarlin and J. L. Lowe left 351 records, July to September 1930 (208 in August), 289 of them at MICH. The names appear together in several strings, and Lowe alone is split over about nine forms (`Lowe,J.L._;_et_al.` 265, "J. L. Lowe" 16, "J.L. Lowe" 11, "J. Lowe" 9 and smaller). What the occasion was, the records don't say; a University of Michigan survey is a guess from memory, consistent with MICH holding most of it, not checked.

1972-08-25. One day, 366 records, seven named collectors: Wetmore 149, S. C. Tucker 124 (plus forms), J. W. Thomson 52, T. H. Nash 16 (and 3 the next day), R. S. Egan 9, P. K. Armstrong 11 (two forms), L. Tibell 1, and one "unknown". Most of the records sit on two points near 48.12 N, 88.57 W (120 and 105 records), and they went to several herbaria (MIN 121, LSU 108, WIS 68, ASU 22, SBBG 18). The records do not say what the occasion was. Alan knows nothing of it (2026-11-02).

1976 to 1977. J. A. Malachowski, 459 records: 4 in October 1976, 411 in June 1977, 44 in July 1977; 450 at MSC. 384 of them are Usnea, all 1977. His 69 records with county "Isle_Royale_NP" are all June and July 1977, MSC.

Smaller: Nancy E. Haefer, 1969 (17 records in seven forms); Muegel ("Dr. Muegel"), 1933, 2 records; H. A. Imshaug, 2 (below); "W.M. Wetmore", 1958, probably C. M. mistyped.

## The taxa table

Its five leading names are all Usnea (subfloridana 180, hirta 92, dasopoga 77, fulvoreagens 74, cavernosa 50). 384 of the cell's Usnea records are Malachowski's, from 1977; Wetmore has 89, Lowe's party about 17. So the table reflects one collector's focus more than the island's lichens: the same lesson as Colorado's apex, where S. Shushan's distributed gatherings of 1961-07-03 head the taxa table.

## The Imshaug packets (2026-11-02)

Two MSC records of Chrysothrix caesia under Imshaug, MSC0032977 (county Eaton, undated) and MSC0032981 (county Allegan, 1957-09-01), both counties in southern Michigan, carry coordinates on Isle Royale: 48.087737, -88.620168. That point holds 55 records, and it isn't a GPS reading: Wetmore's 1959-07-02 records at MSC, ASU, NY and WIS sit on it, and so does a 1930 Lowe record at MICH. It is a shared georeference for one place on the island.

The Imshaug pair are neighbours in MSC's catalogue of the point's source: MSC0032965 is Wetmore's Chrysothrix caesia from 1959-07-02, Keweenaw, and the Imshaug numbers follow a dozen later. Three packets of one species, catalogued in sequence, presumably filed together. The likely story (inferred, not checked): a georeferencing pass worked through MSC's records in catalogue or folder order and carried the Isle Royale point onto the next two packets of the same species. If so, the counties are right and the coordinates wrong; the verbatim counties "Eaton" and "Allegan" look like the labels' own words. MSC0032965 itself is the one record on the point with a blank verbatim county and a blank mark.

It is MSC's error, not Alan's curation, so it is left alone in the data. It moves two records into the cell out of 3,270 and changes nothing about the apex. It is a clean, small example of coordinates travelling by batch, which MSC might want to hear of.

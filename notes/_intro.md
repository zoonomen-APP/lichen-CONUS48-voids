apex_place: how to read a sheet

Each sheet divides a state into hexagonal cells about 18 km across. A cell is
one of two things and never both.

Cyan means the cell holds at least one record, with brightness giving the
number of specimens on a log scale. Plasma — blue through yellow — means the
cell holds no record, with colour giving the distance to the nearest cell that
does.

The two are separate measurements with no conversion between them. The eye
fuses them into a single sense of how well the ground is known, which is a
fair reading, but only as an ordering: the map supports "this is better known
than that" and does not support arithmetic.

The red diamond marks the centre of the largest record-free circle, and the
blue diamond the cell holding the most specimens. Either is omitted where it
would mark a tie rather than a standout.

An empty cell does not mean nobody has collected there. It means no record
meeting the study requirements was produced — the specimen may be
undatabased, held without coordinates, too vaguely localised to place, or
determined only to genus.

Use the selector to choose a state, or the arrow keys to step through all
forty-eight.
